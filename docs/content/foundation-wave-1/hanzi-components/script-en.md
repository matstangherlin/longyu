### 3. Now put them together  `DEMONSTRATE`

**Locução:** On the left, 人, person. On the right, 木, tree. Together they form 休: a person beside a tree, resting.

**Em tela:** hànzì 休 · pinyin xiū · significado "to rest" · peças 人 (person) + 木 (tree)

**Áudio mandarim:** `休`

**Direção:** Peças 人 + 木 entram separadas e depois se juntam em 休; o áudio em mandarim toca aqui (休).

**Tempo estimado:** 00:00:17.929 → 00:00:25.143

### 4. Look at what you just did  `NOTICE`

**Locução:** You read a new character by spotting two shapes you had just seen. That is how Chinese writing stops being thousands of separate drawings and becomes a system. You don't need to memorise 休 — you need to notice this is possible.

**Em tela:** hànzì 休 · peças 人 (person) + 木 (tree)
