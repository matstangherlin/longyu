### 4. Three things, not one  `NOTICE`

**Locução:** 你好 is the writing, called hànzì. nǐ hǎo shows the pronunciation, and it is called pinyin. Hello is the meaning. Every new piece of Mandarin will carry those three layers.

**Em tela:** hànzì 你好 · pinyin nǐ hǎo · significado "Hello"
