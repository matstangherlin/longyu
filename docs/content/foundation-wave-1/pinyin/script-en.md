### 2. Why pinyin exists  `EXPLAIN`

**Locução:** Mandarin is written with hànzì, and looking at a hànzì does not tell you how to say it. Pinyin fixes that: Latin letters that record the sound.

### 4. Pinyin is not a translation  `NOTICE`

**Locução:** This is the most common beginner mix-up. The translation of 你好 is Hello. nǐ hǎo translates nothing — it shows how it sounds. Pinyin is a map: it takes you to the pronunciation, but it is not the place.

**Em tela:** hànzì 你好 · pinyin nǐ hǎo · significado "Hello"
